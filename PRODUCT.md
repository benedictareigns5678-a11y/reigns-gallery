# PRODUCT.md — Suleiman Sallah, website portfolio

register: brand

## What this is
A portfolio of live websites designed and built by Suleiman Sallah. Each work
opens the real site in its own tab. Clients receive one link
(reigns-gallery.vercel.app), and the portfolio itself is the proof of craft.

## Users
Prospective clients (founders, brand owners, marketing leads) deciding whether
to commission a website, usually browsing once, on a laptop or a phone.

## Job of the page
Make them take the work seriously within seconds (the colour wall shows every
site at once), let them go deep on the best pieces (the colour rooms), find
work like theirs (the index, by kind of brand), and start a conversation with
their own colour in hand (the 18th band).

## Honesty
The brands are concept work, and the copy says so ("A concept ..."). No
invented clients, numbers, prices or timelines.

## Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Data lives in js/works.js only.
- Every work's live link must survive any redesign.
- Adding a work is a data entry, not a redesign.
